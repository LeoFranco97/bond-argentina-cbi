The timing

Europe has told the Caribbean to close.

On 25 June 2026 the European Commission wrote to five Caribbean states asking them to phase out their citizenship by investment programmes by 1 June 2028. The letter named Antigua and Barbuda, Dominica, Grenada, Saint Kitts and Nevis, and Saint Lucia. If they do not comply, their citizens can lose visa-free entry to the Schengen Area.

The legal basis is the revised EU Visa Suspension Mechanism, in force since 31 December 2025, under which simply running a citizenship by investment programme is now grounds on its own to suspend visa-free travel.

Those five programmes are what Argentina is about to compete with. Entry there starts near USD 200,000. Argentina starts at USD 350,000, with a passport that was strong before the programme existed and will be strong without it.

The terms

No physical presence

No renunciation

Argentina permits dual citizenship. You keep what you already hold.

No automatic Argentine tax residency

A February 2026 reform moved citizens naturalised through investment out of automatic tax residency. Residency now follows physical presence, not the passport.

On tax, read this carefully

The reform still awaits authorisation from the Ministry of Economy and has no activation date. Two readings of the text compete, one of which treats twelve months of physical presence as the trigger for full worldwide taxation. Treat your own position with a tax adviser. We will not tell you this programme is a tax plan.

Questions

What people ask us.

Can I apply today?
No. Pricing has been announced and the government is targeting the fourth quarter of 2026, but applications are not open, no portal exists, and the implementing regulation has not been published. Anyone offering to file an application for you today cannot do so.
What is the minimum, all in?
USD 350,000 as a non-refundable contribution to the Argentine Treasury for a single applicant. A family of four costs USD 500,000. A bond route at USD 800,000 also exists, but it ties up more than twice the capital for the same passport and is rarely the right answer. Government fees have not been published, so no one can yet quote you a true all-in figure.
Do I have to live in Argentina?
No. The framework qualifies investors regardless of residence and sets no minimum stay.
Do I have to give up my current citizenship?
No. Argentina permits dual citizenship.
Will I become an Argentine taxpayer?
Not through citizenship alone. A February 2026 reform moved investment-naturalised citizens out of automatic tax residency, tying it to physical presence instead. The reform still awaits ministerial authorisation and its wording is read two ways. Take advice on your own position.
How long will approval take?
The National Directorate of Migration is to decide within 30 business days of receiving the vetting reports. That is one stage, not the end-to-end timeline, which has not been published.
Can my children be included?
Yes. Children under 18 are added at USD 25,000, and unmarried children aged 18 to 25 without children of their own at USD 100,000. A spouse is USD 100,000.
What happens to my place if the programme never opens?
Nothing is owed and nothing is paid. A priority registration is a position in our queue and a review of your eligibility, not a transaction. If Argentina does not proceed, we will tell you, and we will tell you what else fits.
